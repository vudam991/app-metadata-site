# SleepySheepie

A calm bedtime companion for relaxation, sleep, and smoother nights.

SleepySheepie is designed to make bedtime feel simpler and more peaceful. The app focuses on a quiet, low-friction experience so users can wind down without extra setup or distractions.

## Features

- No account required.
- Calm, bedtime-focused experience.
- Simple design built for ease of use.
- Designed to support a smoother nighttime routine.

## Why SleepySheepie

SleepySheepie keeps the experience lightweight and approachable, making it easy to open the app and use it right away.
