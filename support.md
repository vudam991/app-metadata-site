# SleepySheepie Support

If you need help with SleepySheepie, have a bug to report, or want to share feedback, contact:

**Email:** iam@no-benefit.com

## About SleepySheepie

SleepySheepie is a simple bedtime companion designed to help users wind down with a calm, focused experience.

## Common questions

- The app does not require an account or sign-in.
- The app does not currently offer subscriptions or in-app purchases.
- If the app is not behaving as expected, please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue when contacting support.

## Response time

We aim to respond as soon as possible.
