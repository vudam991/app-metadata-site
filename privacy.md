# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 16, 2026

SleepySheepie is designed to help users with a calm bedtime routine and does not require account creation or sign-in.

## Information we collect

SleepySheepie does not intentionally collect personal information from users through the app. If you contact support by email, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

## How we use information

If you contact support, we use the information you provide only to respond to your question, troubleshoot problems, or provide app support. SleepySheepie does not currently use account data, subscriptions, or purchase information because those features are not part of the app at this time.

## Sharing and storage

SleepySheepie does not intentionally share personal information with third parties through the app. Information you send by email is stored and handled only as needed for support purposes.

## Your choices

Because SleepySheepie does not require an account, there is generally no account data to access, update, or delete. If you have contacted support and want us to delete your email correspondence, contact us at iam@no-benefit.com. You may also stop using the app at any time by uninstalling it.
